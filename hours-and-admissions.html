Hours and Admissions

Operating Hours

Tuesday – Sunday 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM
Friday Late Nights 9:00 AM – 9:00 PM
Mondays Closed

Last gallery entry is 45 minutes prior to closing. The museum is closed on New Year's Day, Maundy Thursday, Good Friday, and Christmas Day.

General Admission

Adults (Ages 18–59) ₱300
Students & Educators (with valid ID) ₱150
Senior Citizens & PWDs (with valid ID) ₱150
Children (Ages 5 & Under) (must be accompanied by an adult) Free
Museum Members Free Unlimited Entry

Ticket Information & Policies

  • Enjoy free general admission on the first Sunday of every month.
  • Timed-entry tickets may be booked online in advance. Walk-in tickets are available at the main lobby desk subject to daily gallery capacity.
  • General admission grants access to all permanent collection galleries. Select temporary exhibitions may require a separate upgrade pass.
