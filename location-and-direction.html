Location and Directions

Museum Address

Address Tagaytay–Nasugbu Highway, Brgy. Maharlika West, Tagaytay City, 4120 Cavite
Landmark 5 minutes west of Tagaytay Rotonda, overlooking Taal Lake

How to Get Here

By Private Car (via SLEX / CALAX)

Take SLEX and exit at Sta. Rosa or connect to CALAX toward Silang East. Continue straight along Santa Rosa–Tagaytay Road until you reach the Tagaytay Rotonda, then turn right onto Tagaytay–Nasugbu Highway. The museum entrance is on the left side after 1.5 km.

By Private Car (via Aguinaldo Highway)

Head south along Emilio Aguinaldo Highway passing Imus, Dasmariñas, and Silang straight up to Tagaytay Rotonda. Turn right toward Nasugbu/Alfonso and follow the ridge road for 5 minutes.

By Public Commute (Bus / Jeepney)

From PITX (Parañaque Integrated Terminal Exchange), board a bus bound for Nasugbu, Mendez, or Alfonso via Tagaytay. Ask the conductor to drop you off directly at the museum gate along Tagaytay–Nasugbu Highway, or take a local jeepney/tricycle from Olivarez Plaza.

Parking & Drop-Off

  • Complimentary open-air and basement parking is available on-site for museum guests during operating hours.
  • Designated drop-off bays for ride-hailing vehicles, tourist vans, and PWDs are located right at the Main Lobby Courtyard.
